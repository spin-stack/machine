What re-measured `checkpointDowntime` lived only on `exp/415-downtime`, and `lab dispatch` took no `check:TARGET`, so that run was dispatched with `gh workflow run`. *Built:* `TestACheckpointPausesTheGuestBriefly` (`task boot:checkpoint-pause`, lab.yml's `check:checkpoint-pause`), from the experiment with its arms gone. It uses the two guests that decided 30 ms, saves each 10 times at the machine's own setting, continues and syncs after each, and logs the guest's stop to `GuestRunning`, QEMU's downtime, what was left at the stop and the total. The median of the larger of the stop and QEMU's downtime must stay under 50 ms. That is the 30 ms QEMU plans plus 20 for what it writes past the plan and for `cont`. QEMU's default read 199 and 231 ms. compat.yml runs it on every change to QEMU on main and before every release. `lab dispatch check:TARGET` now sends the check, waits, prints the last 60 lines of the `lab:ci` step from the job's log through the API, and exits 1 unless the run succeeded. *Measured* (bench, nested KVM, c8id.4xlarge, v20261121.01, spin's shape plugged to 2 GiB, 10 saves a guest, dispatched with `lab dispatch check:checkpoint-pause`): idle 13.3 ms p50 from the stop to `cont` returned, the slowest 14.8, QEMU's downtime 6 ms, nothing left at the stop, total 465 ms. Writer 18.9 ms p50, the slowest 25.8, downtime 7 ms, total 531 ms. Both agree with the experiment's 14 and 18 ms. *Found on the way:* on the laptop, with its disk busy and its swap full, the writer's pause reached 0.1-8.7 s in 4 and 6 of two runs' 10 saves, with at most 85 MiB left at the stop. QEMU's downtime was longer than the stop to `GuestRunning` (8.68 s against 5.81 s), so QEMU stopped the guest long before the host heard of it at `pre-switchover`. That is #426, and it is why the bound holds the larger of the two. *Status:* in place.
