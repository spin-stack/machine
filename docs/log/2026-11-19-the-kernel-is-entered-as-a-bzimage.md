KASLR was inactive under PVH. The PVH entry into `vmlinux` skips `arch/x86/boot/compressed`, where KASLR picks the random base and sets `KASLR_FLAG`, so `CONFIG_RANDOMIZE_BASE` and `RANDOMIZE_MEMORY` did nothing: every PVH boot had `_text` at `ffffffff81000000`. The machine now boots `kernel/bzImage` (LZ4, as the config already had: x86 has no `KERNEL_UNCOMPRESSED`), which qboot loads from fw_cfg itself (`boot_from_fwcfg`); `linuxboot_dma.bin`, the ROM QEMU adds for a bzImage and SeaBIOS runs, ships again. `vmlinux` and `pvh.bin` stay, for `--kernel vmlinux`, debugging and BTF. A tree without a bzImage (every release before this one) still boots its `vmlinux`, which boot:compat needs. Two builds, the second on a fresh BuildKit with no cache, gave the same bzImage (`ff9f0ec6…`) and vmlinux (`ff53d597…`). *Checked:* `boot:lockdown` now requires `_text` present and not `ffffffff81000000`, and no `nokaslr`; it fails on the PVH tree. Three bzImage boots: `_text` `ffffffffb5c00000`, `ffffffffa2a00000`, `ffffffff90800000`, the physical base moving too (`/proc/iomem`); the PVH `vmlinux`, three times `ffffffff81000000`. The fingerprint does not move (`2bd7da6b…` either way) and `boot:roms` passes: on q35 an option ROM is a fw_cfg file, not a RAM block. *Cost on a laptop (i9-13900HK), a lead only that the bench fleet decides:* `boot:firmware-stages` REPS=5, QEMU exec to the diagnostic init 105.6 vs 83.6 ms p50 summed (qboot copying the bzImage 2.3 ms; the rest the decompressor and the kernel after it); kernel start to init over 10 boots 34-92 ms, p50 ~51, against 34-37 for PVH, so the spread is the bzImage's and not yet explained. `boot:ssh-first` alternated A/B/A/B/A/B at REPS=5: first login 500/658/603 vs 504/587/764 ms p50. The laptop's noise is larger than the difference. Kept whatever the bench fleet says: it is a security fix, and what it costs is the next thing to make cheaper.

Bench, nested (2026-11-19): it costs nothing measurable. `boot:initcalls` in the workspace shape, kernel start to "Freeing" 94.5 → 93.9 ms (lab 44577696958): the laptop's 34-92 ms spread did not reproduce. `boot:ssh-first` against the PVH release, sshd accepts 199 → 198 ms and first login 328 → 317 (lab 44583379559, 20 each); the login gain is in accept-to-login and is main's, not the bzImage's. The LZ4 decompression and the fw_cfg load do not show.
