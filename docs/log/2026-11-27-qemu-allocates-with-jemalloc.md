*Why:* musl's malloc has one lock for every thread and gives memory back a page group at a time, and QEMU's start spent ~2.3 of its main thread's 17 ms of CPU in it on a laptop (#669). *Arms* (#830): QEMU 11.2.0-rc1 still has meson's `malloc` option, and Alpine 3.24 ships `libjemalloc.a` (jemalloc-static 5.3.0) and `libmimalloc.a` (mimalloc2 2.2.7), which meson does not offer and was linked with `--whole-archive`. Each arm's build asked its binary which allocator it ran (jemalloc's `stats_print`, mimalloc's `MIMALLOC_VERBOSE`): a static link takes whichever malloc it reaches first, and a test program whose allocations the compiler elided linked jemalloc and ran musl's. *Measured* (bench, spin's shape and login): against the release, 60 a side, first login −8.6 [−12.3, −5.1] jemalloc and −4.9 [−7.9, −0.9] mimalloc, QEMU's RSS +7.0 and +10.4 MiB (lab 46037242215); restored, a load in full, −3.4 [−4.7, −1.5] and −2.5 [−4.1, −0.4] evicted (lab 46037251403); a fast load's login within noise for both (lab 46037246904). The arms are parts builds (46035803657, 46032713675) and the release compiles its own, so main's parts QEMU at the release's commit (46027613031) went beside them: within noise of the release, −3.0 [−7.5, +1.8], and jemalloc against it −2.6 [−6.8, +0.8] at 60 a side (labs 46039599786, 46039603699). At 150 a side, jemalloc against that build alone: first login −8.6 [−11.0, −6.6], sshd accepting −8.6 [−10.9, −6.7], RSS +6.8 MiB (lab 46040582036); restored −3.4 [−6.9, −0.4] evicted, cached within noise, 60 a side (lab 46040587126). *Done:* `jemalloc-static` in qemu/apk.list, on a builder made for it; `--enable-malloc=jemalloc`, and the build refuses a binary that prints no jemalloc statistics. mimalloc rejected. The licence texts of the libraries linked statically, jemalloc's among them, are #833. *Status:* carried.
