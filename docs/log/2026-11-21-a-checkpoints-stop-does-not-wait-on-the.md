On a busy disk, a checkpoint stopped the guest for 0.1-8.7 s on the laptop, and QEMU's downtime ran past the stop to `GuestRunning`. *Traced* (QEMU `-trace` on `runstate_set`, `vm_stop_flush_all`, `migrate_set_state` and `luring_co_submit`): from `finish-migrate` to `pre-switchover`, the time was `do_vm_stop`'s flush after the drain, one io_uring fsync of the overlay. It took 527 ms in one laptop save. On the bench, beside one host writer on `/tmp`, which is an overlayfs there, and with a guest that synced its own writes, nothing reproduced: Δ +0.9 [−2.6, +5.5] (lab 44957455283). Four writers on the instance store's ext4, with the guest writing its disk with `oflag=direct`, gave the fsync 13-79 ms and the guest's stop 42 ms p50 (lab 44958247487). *Built:* `qemu/patches/0003` adds the migration capability `x-defer-disk-flush`, and `Checkpoint` sets it. After the stop's drain, QEMU's own flushes write to the page cache only, and the guest's next flush forces them. The first version ended the deferral when the switchover ended, and the fsync came back in `cont`, since activating a qcow2 node closes and reopens it. The guest's stop to `GuestRunning` went from 19.6 to 50.3 ms p50 (lab 44958247487). The deferral now lasts until the VM runs (`vm_prepare_start`) or QEMU closes the disks (`bdrv_close_all`). *Measured* (bench, nested KVM, v20261121.01 with QEMU from parts run 44961949665, spin's shape plugged to 2 GiB, the busy-disk guest, 20 pairs interleaved on one guest, branch `exp-defer-ab`): the guest stopped at least 40.0 ms p50 (93.0 slowest) without the capability and 18.0 (28.8) with it, −22.0 [−29.2, −15.8]. QEMU's downtime went from 40 to 7 ms, −33 [−40, −27]. On QEMU's clock, `finish-migrate` to `running` read 8-20 ms against 20-95. `boot:checkpoint-pause` gains the busy-disk guest on the scratch disk, and read idle 13.9, writer 17.3 and busy disk 19.1 ms p50 (lab 44962720691). record and rollback pass on the patched QEMU (lab 44962720561, 44962720776). restore-first, with the spin-machine held at the release's and the QEMU binary as the only change: login −0.1 [−2.3, +2.4] evicted, −3.2 [−4.7, −0.5] cached (lab 44963610565). Main's spin-machine against the release's read +2.2 [+0.5, +4.0] evicted with the release's QEMU (lab 44963610916). That is #438, and it is why the run with both changes read +2.8 (lab 44962720850). *Contract:* a checkpoint's disk is in the page cache when `Checkpoint` returns, as its state is. A caller that keeps the sealed layer syncs it with the state after `Continue` (spin-stack/spin#1514). *Not fixed:* the laptop's other waits, 1.6-1.9 s in the drain with nothing to flush and 0.7-1.6 s for the last RAM, with its swap full (#437). *Status:* in place.
