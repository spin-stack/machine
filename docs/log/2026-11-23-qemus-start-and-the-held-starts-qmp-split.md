#611's split left two cold phases without an owner. *#649, the held start's 14 commands, 6.1 ms on bench:* QEMU waited 4.5 ms p50 for the client, 0.9 of it busy with the probe's plug monitor (the memory spin sizes right after the start), so spin-machine's own share is ~3.6 ms, ~0.28 ms a round trip (24 boots, lab 45177818419). The candidate sent the bind ahead of its answers (`qmp.Send`): the run state with the places, every serial at once, each image's two nodes together, the reopen with the placeholder's delete, 13 round trips to 9; add-fd still holds the connection until its answer and `cont` waits for the bind. *Measured* (bench, nested, spin's shape and login, v20261123.06's parts, spin-machine main at f273377 against the candidate at c966407, 60 a side): first login −1.7 [−5.6, +2.6], sshd accepting −2.4 [−5.5, +2.8] (lab 45230790902); restored login −0.3 [−3.8, +3.7] evicted, +0.9 [−10.6, +7.5] cached (lab 45231358105). Rejected: the four round trips it removes are ~1.1 ms (inferred), and even all of spin-machine's 3.6 ms is under what 60 boots a side resolve. The code is on `archive/exp-649-held-start`. *#647, QEMU's exec to its first trace event, 9.7 ms on bench:* the release's QEMU is static-pie on musl, so there is no dynamic loader and `LD_DEBUG` has nothing to report; musl relocates its 59,700 relative relocations itself. The split, on the laptop as a lead (exec to the first `flatview_new`, 40 starts a row, 4.7-5.4 ms p50): a trivial static-pie binary 0.23, QEMU's `-version` 2.0, `-sandbox` −0.7 to −0.9, the rest option parsing, trace and main-loop start and the machine object. perf on an unstripped build of the same Dockerfile: relocation 7% of `-version`, page faults ~15%, nothing else over 2%. No candidate's ceiling is over ~2 ms on bench (inferred at ~2× the laptop): a seccomp filter compiled at build time (Firecracker's seccompiler), a non-PIE binary (which gives up ASLR), DT_RELR. None was built. The allocator, musl's mallocng, is ~2.3 of the 17 ms of CPU QEMU's main thread spends from exec to held, a lead across QEMU's whole start, opened as #669. *Status:* rejected, both.
