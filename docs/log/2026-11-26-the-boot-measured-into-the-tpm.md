*Why:* shirudo anchors its register in PCR 9 and wants PCRs that say what booted, and nothing measured this machine's boot: qboot enters a PVH vmlinux QEMU loaded, with no EFI and so no EFI stub, and no TCG log existed (#742). *Prior art:* qboot has no TPM code; SeaBIOS's tcgbios.c measures option ROMs, the MBR and separators, never fw_cfg's kernel, initrd or command line; Stefan Berger's 2011 SeaBIOS series had QEMU hash those three and the firmware extend them (never merged); QEMU's SEV kernel hashes and OVMF measure through UEFI, Kata through TEE launch digests, and Firecracker has no vTPM. *Done:* qboot (`qemu/qboot/tpm.patch`) starts the TPM behind tpm-crb, extends the events the machine names in `opt/org.spin-stack/tpm-events` into every active bank and writes the crypto-agile log into `etc/tpm/log`; `machine.Spec.TPMEvents` names the kernel in PCR 4 (`EV_IPL`) and the command line and initrd in PCR 9 as the EFI stub's tagged events, from `TPM.Kernel` and `TPM.Initrd`, which the caller hashes once a file (`machine.FileSHA256`). qboot hashes nothing: it never sees the vmlinux, and a portable SHA-256 read 60 MB in 400 ms on a laptop. The banks other than SHA-256 take the same digest cut or padded, so none is left at zero. A guest's log has the three events, `sha256sum` of the vmlinux and the initrd and of `/proc/cmdline` equal them, every bank replays to the TPM's PCRs (`boot:tpm`, which fails on the release's qboot), and go-attestation parses and verifies it. *Measured* on the bench, spin's shape, 20 a side: byte-wise copies to the CRB buffer, which KVM cannot map, cost the first login +72.3 [+65.6, +78.9] with the CLI's hashing (lab 45834850060); word-wise +43.9 [+28.0, +57.8] (lab 45837751252); without separators in PCRs 0-7, which cost +13 of it, +28.9 [+20.3, +35.4], of which the CLI's hashing of the vmlinux at every boot is +26.0 [+18.4, +37.2] (lab 45838578696, #776). The firmware's measuring alone, digests taken from the manifest on a branch, was −3.6 [−11.7, +8.8], within noise (lab 45839316754). A machine without a TPM is unchanged (lab 45837751252), and the restored login too (lab 45834864452). Those runs booted v20261126.01's kernel. On main's, kernel 0032 skipped the self test only after a TPM2_Startup the kernel sent itself, so a TPM qboot had started paid the whole self test again: +23.9 [+15.8, +32.7] against main (lab 45840998579). 0032 now probes with TPM2_Startup, which a started TPM refuses with TPM2_RC_INITIALIZE, and sends no self test at all; against main, both with a TPM, the measured machine reads −1.1 [−8.9, +10.5] (lab 45843630680), the restored login +0.8 [+0.0, +2.0] (lab 45843661633), the CLI's hashing +28.1 [+22.8, +37.2], and a machine without a TPM is unchanged. *Status:* carried for every machine with a TPM, with kernel 0032 revised. PCR 11 still holds no phase measurement, so shirudo still warns: systemd-pcrphase asks for a measured UKI (#773).
