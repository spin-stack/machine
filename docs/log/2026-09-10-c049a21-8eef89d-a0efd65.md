QEMU's startup split: 1.4 ms launcher, ~27 ms exec to first instruction, ~7 ms guest to first console byte. The QMP greeting is not a checkpoint: it comes before the board is built, so the real QMP reply is at 18-20 ms, not 8. Cold-start cost is the host zeroing pages (18.8%) plus rom_reset; a restore pays neither (09-26). Not acted on.
