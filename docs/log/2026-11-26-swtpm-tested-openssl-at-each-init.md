*Why:* after #745 a TPM still cost the restored first login +6.3 ms and the cold one ~+10, put on swtpm's start (#775). *Traced* on the bench, strace of swtpm and QEMU on their control channel, 3 a side (labs 45838340907 cold, 45838342840 restored): swtpm's start is not on the path. QEMU sends its first control command 6.3-7.2 ms after its exec and swtpm, started beside it, answers in 0.1 ms. QEMU waits instead on CMD_INIT, 5.0-5.6 ms under strace: at its reset on a cold boot, and on a restore after STOP and the state blobs (0.5 ms), with no INIT at the reset of a QEMU waiting for its stream. Inside the INIT, 1 ms reads the host's openssl.cnf and the rest is CPU, which callgrind (one INIT, swtpm 0.10.2 as qemu/Dockerfile builds it) put 89% of in `tpmlib_maybe_configure_openssl`: RSA signs at every key size, asking whether OpenSSL disabled an algorithm the profile enables. *Done:* qemu/swtpm/openssl-unconfigured.patch initialises swtpm's static libcrypto without a configuration file and skips the test, which nothing outside the binary can then change; the stage holds it with a configuration file that makes an unpatched swtpm refuse to start. Against main's QEMU from the same pipeline, both with a TPM, 30 a side: the cold first login −5.1 [−9.7, −0.6] (lab 45844362398), restored −3.8 [−5.2, −0.8] evicted (lab 45844362132); against the release's QEMU, −2.6 [−7.0, −0.2] and −3.4 [−5.0, −2.1] (labs 45842988805, 45842989011). In the same runs a TPM costs +9.0 cold and +5.7 restored against none, so ~4 and ~2 ms are left: the kernel's refused self test and startup, the capability reads, the state blobs and what remains of the INIT. boot:tpm, boot:lockdown and boot:shirudo-here pass with the patched swtpm (run 45846187264). *Not tried:* starting swtpm earlier or in parallel, since it already is and is waiting when QEMU asks.
