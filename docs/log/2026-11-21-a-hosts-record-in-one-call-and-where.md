spin's runner started a held machine only to read `QEMU.Target` and kept the answer (spin-stack/spin#1416). spin also synced the state file every 100 ms while QEMU wrote it, synced it again inside the seal, and bounded a resume by the state's written blocks. *Built:* `machine.ReadTarget(ctx, Spec, StartOptions)` starts a machine of the Spec held, reads its `Target` and stops it. The caller's monitors are dropped. Each NIC is put on a hub port, so no TAP and no `/dev/vhost-net` is needed. The console writes to nothing, but the serial port is kept. *Measured* (laptop, v20261121.01, spin's shape, 2026-11-21): a machine whose NIC was on a real TAP with vhost read a record byte-identical to `ReadTarget`'s, and 25 589 bytes. One call took 72-76 ms p50 over 20 (QEMU's start 28, the record 11, its quit 37). It runs at a runner's start, off both goal clocks. boot:record now holds a machine's own checkpoint to `ReadTarget`'s record of its shape held for a checkpoint. Its first run failed with `device isa-serial: checkpoint isa-serial, here none`, because the test's Spec had no console. The record names the serial port, so the caller's Spec has to carry the console its machines have. The lab still reads a tree's record through that tree's `spin-machine target`, because the lab can drive a binary that predates `ReadTarget`. `restore` and the probes that resume into a machine they just started still ask that machine, which is the right call when there is no host constant. *Checkpoint sync, decided by measurement* (laptop ext4 NVMe, 99% full and shared; spin's shape plugged to 2 GiB, 1.5 GiB touched, 1.6 GiB written per save, mapped-ram with 8 channels; a seal that creates and syncs a file; 16 interleaved saves an arm, medians over the saves that did not stall on the disk): with the sync inside the seal, the pause's sync was 275 ms and the guest stopped 683 ms from seal to `cont`. Every 100 ms and then inside the seal gave 167 ms and 566. After `cont` gave 0 and 506. Over every save, the sync after `cont` beat the periodic arm in the pause by 183 ms [−1676, −160] and in QEMU's total by 232 [−2027, −124]. The guest's whole pause does not resolve over every save, because the stalls were seconds long and hit every arm. The sync after `cont` took 2-3 ms. QEMU syncs nothing itself (strace). The seal's file took 2-7 ms with ~830 MiB dirty, so spin's reason for the periodic sync did not reproduce on ext4. *Decided:* `Checkpoint` syncs nothing, and the caller fdatasyncs after `Continue`. The resume bound stays with the caller, because it rests on the host's slowest disk. `Resume` is unchanged, so restore-first was not re-run. Both decisions are written down in docs/memory-and-disk.md. `Cost.Paused`, QEMU's downtime, reads 200-250 ms under the caller's clock from the seal to `cont`. That gap is not yet traced. *Status:* in place. spin replaces `qemu.Target` with `ReadTarget` and moves its `fdatasync` after `Continue`/`Hold`.
