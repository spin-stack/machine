The cold login does not wait for logind on v20261122.07. In its ledger (lab 45082187351, 3 boots), logind is active within 1-13 ms of `basic.target`, before or after it. The login's session opens 2-4 ms after it is authenticated, and the session waits 0-5.6 ms for root's manager (`user-early`). The login waits for the forwarder's chain (journald → tmpfiles → `sysinit.target` → `basic.target`) and for the key exchange on a vCPU that idled 14 ms of the 185 from PID 1 to the connection (boot 2). The two arms were lab edits (`logind-weight`, `offpath-weight`), on the bench, spin's shape and login, interleaved against v20261122.07. *logind, `user-runtime-dir@` and `user@` at `StartupCPUWeight=1000`:* first login −9.2 [−16.1, −0.3] at 30 a side in a screening run of three arms (lab 45082870346). The run fixed in advance to decide it, the arm alone at 40 a side, read −3.6 [−10.3, +0.6] (lab 45083184310). Accept to login was −7.7 [−14.0, −4.0] and sshd accepting +8.8 [−1.1, +13.3]. In its ledgers (lab 45083106264), logind is active ~9 ms sooner and `sysinit.target` ~18 ms later (p50 of 3). The weight moves the vCPU from the forwarder's chain to the manager's, and the login needs both. Restored, in noise: −1.2 to +0.1 (lab 45083184131, 20 a side). *`spin-machine-sshd-keygen` and `systemd-udev-load-credentials` at `StartupCPUWeight=1`:* sshd accepting +22.6 [+15.9, +33.5], first login +3.4 [−3.2, +14.6]. ssh.service waits for the keygen, which finished 45-70 ms later (lab 45083106264), and the forwarder waits for ssh.service. The keygen is on spin's path, not off it. Both together +6.0 [+0.5, +15.1]. Under cgroup v2, a weight is shared with the unit's siblings: `user@0` competes in `user-0.slice`, and `user.slice` competes with `system.slice` and `init.scope`. `StartupCPUWeight=` holds until the boot's jobs are done, and on spin's path the getty's wait for `dev-ttyS0` keeps them running past the login. *Decided:* neither is kept, and both rows are in "Measured and rejected". On one busy vCPU, a weight only changes which chain waits. What the login needs before it is the CPU it takes. *Status:* the edits are removed.
