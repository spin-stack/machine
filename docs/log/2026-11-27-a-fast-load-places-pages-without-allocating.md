*Why:* spin's suspend and resume loop stopped for good at 8 of 608 fast loads of the releases' QEMU (kvm.yml 46004238975, 46014308166, 46018501292). The guest's vCPUs waited on pages nobody placed, and QEMU answered no monitor. Every frozen QEMU showed the same cycle. QEMU's main thread was inside musl's malloc, holding its one lock, when io_uring ran a cold disk read's task work on it, on the return from an interrupt or a system call. The read's copy into a guest page not placed yet waited on the userfaultfd. The fault thread waited for that lock: `qemu_get_buffer_at()` allocated an iovec for every page, and the fault thread added each faulting page to `page_requested`, a GTree. The eager thread waited for `page_request_mutex`, which the fault thread held, or for the same lock. *Done:* qemu/patches/0011 reads each page with `qio_channel_pread()`, which allocates nothing, and keeps `qemu_get_buffer_at()`'s check of the stream's error. It places a faulting page without a `page_requested` entry: a fast load has no source to ask, and `pending_bmap` already has each page placed once. postcopy-blocktime no longer counts a fast load's faults. `boot:fastload-reads` holds it, in compat.yml with five loads: a guest reading its disk through fast loads made long and cold (QEMU's reads through io.max, the disk dropped from the host's cache every 100 ms, query-blockstats meanwhile) reads on, and its state is read to its end. Ten loads made only cold took a second or two each and all passed without 0011 (lab 46014214339), which is why the lane makes them long. *Measured:* without 0011, each of three lane runs stopped at its first load, the main thread in `handle_userfault` from io_uring task work and the fault thread on musl's `__malloc_lock` (labs 46015140066, 46016828489, 46016833115). With it, 35 of 35 lane loads read to their end (labs 46015145265, 46016838150, 46019688290, compat 46019149732), and so did 299 of 299 fast loads in spin's loop, run beside 81 of the release's (kvm.yml 46018501292). restore-first, spin's shape and login, touched=1700, 30 a side: login −0.3 [−23.7, +21.5] evicted and +0.4 [−14.5, +21.2] cached, running +0.3 [−1.5, +1.7] and +1.1 [−0.7, +2.1], within noise (lab 46014684857). *Status:* open as #829. A release, and spin's bump to it, are the owner's.
