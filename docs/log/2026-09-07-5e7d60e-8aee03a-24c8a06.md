`cmd/spin-machine-init` is a debug initramfs, and `spin-machine-console.service` is shipped but not enabled. *Why:* systemd gave no serial login, because the image masked udev, so `dev-ttyS0.device` never appeared. *Status:* the init was deleted `8eeb01c` (09-10) and the console unit deleted `3a7864d` (11-12).
