The question was how to make QMP faster, posed as if QMP ran over vsock, with vsock RTT tuning, CPU pinning, `halt_poll_ns`, SEQPACKET, ivshmem, virtio-serial and gRPC framing as candidates. QMP is a Unix socket between the host and QEMU. vsock is between the host and the guest, and carries spin's SSH login. So two questions: how much of each goal number is QMP, and how much is the login's vsock. `transport` boots and restores spin's shape with QEMU traced (`qmp_enter_*`, the monitor's queue and answers, the run state, host time). After the cold login it times query-status, a one-byte echo over vsock and through a forwarder hop, and a warm login's round trips. *Measured* (bench, spin's shape and login, v20261121.01, 20 boots, 0 lost): the cold boot waits on 14 commands, 6.2/6.6 ms from QEMU's main loop being free to `cont` answered, 1.3% of the traced first login's 470. Of that, 4.5 ms is QEMU waiting for spin-machine's next command, and the commands' own run is ~1.2. A restore waits on 17 commands before `migrate-incoming`, 6.7/7.6 ms (5.2 of it waiting on the client), and 2.7 ms from the guest running to the restore's last answer, of a 114 ms first login. spin-machine's start and dial are off both paths: its first command waits 17.1 ms (cold) and 19.5 (restored) for QEMU to finish making the machine. query-status takes 131/142 µs from a Go client and 18 µs inside QEMU. A vsock round trip is 47.8 µs, 57.5 through a forwarder like spin's, and a login is 10 of them: 0.6 ms of a 32.8 ms warm login. *Why not double:* each QMP round trip on the path is ~0.25-0.9 ms where the command runs in ~0.02-0.35. The rest is the client's turn in a process that has just started, on a nested host. *Decided:* no change. If QMP cost nothing, the cold number would gain at most 6.2 ms and the restore at most ~9.4. Pipelining the bind gains at most 4.5 cold and 5.2 restored, and the read-ahead overlaps the restore's half. Dropping the round trips that can go gains ~1.3 cold and ~1.9 restored. Every vsock idea is bounded by 0.6 ms a login. All of these are under the noise at 20 boots (±7-9 ms cold, ±13 restored), so none was built (docs/boot-time.md, "QMP and vsock"). The laptop agreed (i9-13900HK, under load): 14 and 17 commands, vsock 13-16 µs, 10 turns a login. *Status:* the probe is kept so the split can be taken again.
