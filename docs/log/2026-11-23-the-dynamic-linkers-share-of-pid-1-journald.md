#574's runner-up: the four map and relocate `libsystemd-shared` (PID 1 and `user@0` also `libsystemd-core`), and nobody had measured ld.so's share before porting to v262's multicall build (#42304) or a static one (#42820). *Instrument* (branch `exp-ldso`, tagged `archive/exp-ldso`, its `ldso` edit): `LD_DEBUG=statistics` for PID 1 from spin's handoff, which execs it (`systemd.setenv=` does not reach PID 1), and for journald, logind and `user@0` from a drop-in each. PID 1 and journald report on `/dev/kmsg` with `printk.devkmsg=on`, logind and `user@0` on their stderr, the journal: the first run had logind's on `/dev/kmsg` too, its `DevicePolicy=` refused the executor the open, and logind failed at STDERR (lab 45180400607, discarded). glibc 2.43 counts in TSC cycles, 2700 MHz in every boot. *Measured* (bench, nested, spin's shape and login, v20261123.05 on its traceable kernel, the ledger with and without the edit, 8 a side, lab 45180856158), ld.so's total start-up, p50 [range]: PID 1 0.48 [0.46, 0.56] ms, of it loading objects 0.32 and relocation 0.08; journald 0.20 [0.14, 0.31]; logind 0.20 [0.11, 0.31]; `user@0` 0.21 [0.15, 0.36]. Relocations: PID 1 and `user@0` 2,522 symbol (911 from cache) and 16,589 relative; journald 1,157 and 10,518; logind 1,238 and 11,297. Objects at start, from the release's image (`ldd`): 7 for PID 1 and `user@0` (the binary, libsystemd-core, libsystemd-shared, libc, libm, libcrypt, ld.so), 6 for journald and logind, all `BIND_NOW`. The four come to ~1.1 ms, under #638's ~5 ms, so no build was made. *The instrument:* against the ledger alone in the same run, first login +3.4 [−23.0, +11.9], PID 1's CPU −1.1 [−5.1, +0.6], unattributed +0.3 [−0.1, +1.1]; each ld.so total is well inside the trace's wall time from the process's exec to its first sleep. It does not see the kernel's execve before ld.so runs, nor the libraries' pages faulted in later by `main`, which a static build would partly pay too (not measured). `user@0`'s environment generator inherits the variable and reports 0.13 ms more. *Inferred:* the multicall build would not reach even that: it keeps libsystemd-core and libsystemd-shared dynamic, leaves journald and logind alone and makes the executor, static here, dynamic. *Status:* measured and rejected; #638 closed.
