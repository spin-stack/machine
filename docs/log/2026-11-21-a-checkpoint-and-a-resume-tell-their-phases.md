A checkpoint reported only at its end, with `Cost`. spin's snapshot showed nothing until then, and the guest stayed stopped 200-250 ms past `Cost.Paused` for no traced reason (#399). *Built* (#407): `SaveOptions.Phases` and `LoadOptions.Phases` take a func told each typed `machine.Phase` of one call. A save is `SaveStarted`, `RAMWritten` (a `query-migrate` every 100 ms while the guest runs), `GuestStopped`, `Sealed{Took}` and `SaveCompleted{Cost}`, then `GuestRunning` from `Continue` or `GuestHeld` from `Hold`. A load is `LoadStarted`, `StateLoaded{Bytes}` and `GuestRunning`, and after a fast load `Loaded` tells `StateLoaded`. The hook is per call and not per machine, because spin's timeline is per snapshot (spin-stack/spin#1481). Without `Phases`, a save and a load send the same commands as before. `spin-machine save` and `restore` print the timeline. *Observed on real QEMU* (boot:record, laptop, v20261121.01, a 128 MiB guest): a save told SaveStarted at 0, GuestStopped and Sealed at 45.4 ms, SaveCompleted at 50.2 and GuestHeld at 51.0. A load told LoadStarted at 0, StateLoaded at 35.3 and GuestRunning at 35.8. *#399 traced* (laptop ext4, spin's shape with 2 GiB plugged and 1.5 GiB of tmpfs filled, 1.6 GiB a save, 8 channels, every command and event timed): the gap fell between `remove-fd`'s answer and the next command. Each command took under a millisecond, and `cont` 2-3 ms. QEMU `ftruncate`s the state it writes, and ext4's `auto_da_alloc` writes a truncated file's dirty pages back on its last close. That last close was Checkpoint's copy of the file, closed before Checkpoint returned. Closed on a goroutine, with 16 saves an arm interleaved on one guest: `completed` to `cont`'s return fell from 238 ms p50 to 6, and the guest's stop to `cont` from 451 to 198, −252 [−4354, −33]. The caller's `fdatasync` after `Continue` rose from 3 ms to 259, so the time to a durable state did not change (−19 [−4088, +3963]). The phases also showed that the last `RAMWritten` before the stop was 0.3-0.9 GiB short of `Cost.RAM`. QEMU writes that RAM after the seal, with the guest stopped (#415). *Measured* (bench, spin's shape and login, v20261121.01's spin-machine against this branch's at cd966d5, which also carries main since the release; 20 each, interleaved): restore-first's login +2.5 [−0.2, +6.1] evicted and −2.3 [−5.0, +1.7] cached, within noise; `running` +2.3 [+1.7, +3.5] evicted and −0.1 [−1.2, +1.3] cached (#416). ssh-first's first login +0.3 [−4.9, +7.1], within noise. `StateLoaded.Bytes`, one `fstat`, came after these runs. *Status:* in place. spin takes `Phases` into its snapshot timeline, and syncs the state after `Continue` as before.
