#437 guessed that the laptop's slowest saves, 1.6-1.9 s in the drain and 0.7-1.6 s for the last RAM, were QEMU's memory read back from a full swap. *Traced on the laptop* (QEMU `-trace` with wall-clock timestamps, each thread's `/proc` state, `wchan` and major faults every 2 ms): QEMU had nothing in swap and the host swapped in 167 pages in 11 minutes. The waits were a disk 60-80% full in `/proc/pressure/io`: guest writes in flight in `jbd2_log_wait_commit` and `rq_qos_wait`, which the drain waited for, and major faults on QEMU's own binary, evicted by reclaim (1.9 M file refaults) and read back from the disk that held the state, the overlay, the swap and the busy writers. A run without the busy writers, the disk still saturated by other work, waited 1.5 s once in the caller before `cont`. *Runners* have no swap (only spin's control plane gets a swap file, and it is never a runner) and give each machine's group `memory.swap.max` 0. *On the bench*, no swap and QEMU's binary on a disk apart from the state: with the host's memory taken but 1 GiB, the busy disk's dirty pages got the test killed. With 4 GiB left, the writer read 23.0 ms p50 (43.5 slowest) and with the busy disk 26.1 (44.4), against 18.5 beside the busy disk alone. QEMU took no major fault while the host reclaimed 26 M pages. `bpftrace` on `sched_switch` named the two slowest of 40 saves (139 and 188 ms): `balance_dirty_pages`, on a guest write in the drain and on one multifd channel's write of the last RAM, the other seven channels behind that file's `i_rwsem`. *Tried:* `direct-io` on the save's channels, with an `O_DIRECT` descriptor added to the state's set. Beside the busy disk it never converged, each pass ~2.5 s and ~520 MiB left, and was cancelled after 10 min. Rejected. `downtime-limit` 15: −6.0 [−11.9, −1.1] in 20 pairs short of memory, then −2.5 [−7.5, +3.0] in 25, −1.5 [−6.0, +2.8] with the writer, −0.2 [−0.5, +0.3] idle. Rejected. *Built:* `boot:checkpoint-pause`'s fourth guest is the busy-disk guest on a host whose available memory is taken but 4 GiB (`shortOfMemory`), measured and not held to the bound: on compat's kvm host, 118 GiB of it taken, the stop read 56.2 ms p50 against 25.2 on the bench (compat 44976542939, lab 44976542360). *Status:* the test is in place. A runner does not reach #437's seconds. Its stop on a host short of memory is the dirty limit's (#444).
