`Continue` right after `Checkpoint` failed once in about 235 checkpoints with `cont: GenericError: Migration is not finalized yet`, and the guest stayed stopped. *Explained* (QEMU 11.2.0-rc0's `migration/migration.c`): the migration thread sets `completed`, sends `MIGRATION{completed}` and lets the BQL go in `migration_completion_end`, and moves the run state from `finish-migrate` to `postmigrate` only once it holds the BQL again, in `migration_iteration_finish`. `qmp_cont` refuses `finish-migrate` (`monitor/qmp-cmds.c`), and `runstate_set` sends no event. `blockdev-set-active` is not refused there, so a `Hold` in the window succeeded and left the machine in `finish-migrate`. *Reproduced* (laptop, real KVM, v20261121.01 with this checkout's spin-machine, spin's shape plugged to 2 GiB, a guest rewriting 600 MiB of tmpfs, Checkpoint then Continue in a loop): 0 refusals in 2000 checkpoints on an idle host. With QEMU and the test pinned to two host CPUs kept busy by two spin loops, 102 of 300 `cont`s were refused, `query-status` reading `finish-migrate` at the refusal, and with `Hold` first, 88 of 300 Holds left the machine in `finish-migrate` and 79 `cont`s were refused. *Built:* `Checkpoint` asks `query-status` after the cost's `query-migrate`, and again every millisecond while it reads `finish-migrate`, bounded by its context. Making `Continue` retry `cont` was rejected: `Hold` and `Status` would still see `finish-migrate`. *Measured:* under the same contention, 0 of 300 refused, and 0 of 300 with `Hold` first. The guest's stop to `GuestRunning`, 8 runs of 60 checkpoints an arm interleaved on the idle laptop, moved from 6.62 ms p50 to 6.82, +0.20 [+0.08, +0.33], one QMP round trip. *Status:* in place. A checkpoint is on neither goal clock.
