Nothing checked that a rollback brings back the same memory and disk, byte for byte. boot:record checked that a counter goes on after a restore, and boot:compat that a release's checkpoint resumes. *Built:* `TestARollbackBringsBackTheSameMemoryAndDisk` (`task boot:rollback`, lab.yml's `check:rollback`). It boots spin's shape plugged to 2 GiB to systemd, logs in as root over SSH through vsock, and runs the maintainer's terminal loop `while true; do date +%T.%N; sleep 0.1; done` detached, beside a Perl process that holds 4 MiB of random bytes and a counter and stamps each line with both of the guest's clocks on the serial console. Then it takes four steps. A checkpoint with memory is sealed as spin's storage seals a disk and continued in place. A rollback with memory goes onto the sealed disk after the guest changed the file and the bytes. A disk-only snapshot is `stop`, `blockdev-snapshot` and `cont`. A cold boot comes from the sealed disk. Left at 128 MiB, the guest's OOM killer took the SSH session, as the lab's Plug says. *Observed* (v20261121.01, 3 runs on the laptop and 1 on the bench, which agreed). The checkpoint stopped the guest 11-17 ms (`Cost.Paused` 11-14), inside one of the loop's sleeps, so the loop's largest step stayed at 101-105 ms. After the rollback the process had the same PID and the same bytes. The file matched from memory and again from the disk. The loop and the counter went on from their lines at the checkpoint, and the kernel reseeded its RNG (vmgenid). The cold boot had the synced file and not the later one. The loop's log, written 3 s and never synced, was not on the disk at all, because the seal is crash-consistent. The disk-only snapshot stopped the guest 7-14 ms. The guest's clocks stop with it. A stop held 1.00 s left the guest's wall clock 1.001 s further behind, and both clocks stepped 101 ms across it. The resumed guest read the checkpoint's time, 6.34 s behind 48 ms after the resume, and nothing in the machine corrected it, by the 09-10 decision. spin steps it at the supervisor's next registration, and after a snapshot continued in place nothing steps it (spin-stack/spin#1498). An SSH session open at the checkpoint did not survive the rollback. The restored sshd read `Transport endpoint is not connected` within 3 ms, its pty shell was gone at the first look, and the guest's kernel logged nothing of vhost-vsock's reset (spin-stack/spin#1483). *Status:* in place. `lab dispatch` takes no `check:TARGET` yet (#419).
