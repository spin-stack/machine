That is correctness and stays. *What the manager does* (the ledger, lab 45062836688, v20261122.05, 3 boots, CPU before the login's end, p50): `user@0` 17.5 ms in all; systemd-executor and the PAM stack of `systemd-user` 4.6-5.0, of it `unix_chkpwd` 1.1; the manager from its exec to its generators 2.6; the environment.d generator 2.0; the XDG autostart generator 1.6; loading units 4.4; the first transaction to `Startup finished` 1.4. A workspace needs the environment.d generator (it reads `10-user-sockets.conf`, the bus's address for user services, and `/etc/environment`), the sockets, and the PAM stack's account check, limits and audit uid.
