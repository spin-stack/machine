*Why:* shirudo, a guest-side enforcer of BPF LSM programs with its register in a TPM, was published on 2026-11-23, and it attaches to LSM hooks no released kernel has. 7.4-rc4 brought the BPF signed loader's ABI it loads its programs through (#720); its own hooks are still shirudo's alone. *Done:* kernel/patches 0026-0031 are shirudo's six LSM and BPF patches (`task_cgroup_attach`, `tpm_command`, `security.bpf.` label reads, sleepable inode hooks, a trusted `d_parent`, `mm_read_access`), regenerated with `git format-patch -N` on 7.4-rc4 over this series; all six applied as they are, the same change as upstream's by patch id. 7.4-rc4 already has the x86 ftrace fix shirudo asks for (31ac959c915c), so nothing carries it. shirudo's kbuild and riscv patches are declined in `internal/build/shirudo.go`, each with its reason. `versions.yaml`'s `shirudo` pins the commit they come from, and `spin-machine-dev bump shirudo` compares upstream's patches with the carried ones by Subject and fails naming every one a person has to answer. Its first run found nothing to answer once it skipped format-patch's signature, which one of shirudo's patches lacks. Each patch has its assertion in `kernel/Dockerfile`, and a build without the six fails naming each. The kernel turns on `CONFIG_TCG_TPM`, `CONFIG_TCG_CRB` and `CONFIG_TCG_TIS`, with what olddefconfig derives from them in config-7.4-x86_64: sparse compiles every C file the patches touch, and 0027's, the TPM character device, was in no build without them; without a TPM device the driver binds to nothing. *Not yet:* shirudo also needs a TPM in the machine (#719) and dm-verity, ML-DSA and system data verification turned on (`CONFIG_BLK_DEV_DM`, `CONFIG_DM_VERITY`, `CONFIG_DM_VERITY_VERIFY_ROOTHASH_SIG`, `CONFIG_CRYPTO_MLDSA`, `CONFIG_SYSTEM_DATA_VERIFICATION`, #720). *Status:* in place.
