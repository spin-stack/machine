The ~450 ms that blocked udev after basic.target and spin's forwarder early was not guest work. Sampled on the bench (branch `exp/452-diag`), a slow restore's vCPU thread ran ~17 ms after the resume and then sat in `kvm_vcpu_halt` for 281-450 ms, neither running nor waiting, and the guest's /proc/stat was missing that time. Traced with the host's kvm and vsock events, the guest answered the login's packets until it halted, and woke on its first local timer interrupt, with no TSC-deadline write before it: the slow login ended 528-555 ms after QEMU's exec however long the load took. kprobes on `kvm_synchronize_tsc` showed why. QEMU writes each vCPU's TSC before the load, zero after the vCPU is made and one at the reset, and KVM, having had a TSC from userspace, takes the migration's TSC within a second of it for a sync and keeps the offset (kernel bf328e22e472's case, undone). The guest's TSC counts from the new vCPU's creation, so its saved TSC deadline is met only after the saved uptime, ~0.5 s. A guest saved with its tick running (udev still at work) had that deadline a tick ahead and reprogrammed none of its later timers. One saved idle armed far ahead and reprogrammed, so the release's restores never showed it. The coldplug arm's restored +463.7 (lab 44818953987) was the same. QEMU 0005 writes an incoming machine's TSC only from the migration: the writes became the creation's and the checkpoint's, unmatched, and 16 of 16 restores read 84-94 ms (lab 44987412184). *Measured,* spin's shape, 30 a side against the release: udev after basic.target with 0005 cold −48.6 [−51.7, −42.6], restored −2.0 [−8.0, +4.7] with p95 114 against 114 evicted and 126 against 128 cached; without 0005 the same edit's restored p95 is 539-546 (lab 44987412171). In `prestart`, cold −44.7 [−53.4, −39.4], restored into prestarted −1.3 [−4.1, +3.6] with p95 98.8 against 101, and without 0005 413 (lab 44987412210). 0005 alone moves neither number (cold −3.0 [−5.8, +0.1], restored −1.9 [−7.6, +2.0]). spin's forwarder early with 0005: cold −60.4 [−63.3, −52.9], restored +3.3 [+1.0, +7.4], p95 104 against 101 (lab 44987584707), so it is spin's to take. The image now carries copies of systemd-udevd.service and systemd-udev-trigger.service without `Before=sysinit.target`, and image/build.sh checks them. *A first 0005 skipped only the zero:* the reset's one still set KVM's flag, and the tails stayed (labs 44986055383, 44986006042).
