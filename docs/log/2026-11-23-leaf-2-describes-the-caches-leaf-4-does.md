*Why:* #565 claimed that with `x-consistent-cache=off` leaves 2 and 4 describe the same caches. *Seen in a guest* (bench, nested, spin's shape, v20261123.02, lab 45100482399): leaf 2 EDX 0x2c307d, ECX 0x4d, so glibc read a 2 MiB 8-way L2 (descriptor 0x7D) while leaf 4 and the guest's kernel said 4 MiB 16-way. L1 and L3 agreed, and so did every threshold glibc derives (`shared_cache_size` 16 MiB, `non_temporal_threshold` 8 MiB, the same before #570), because QEMU's legacy L3 is inclusive and glibc adds the L2 to the shared size only for a non-inclusive one. What moved is `sysconf(_SC_LEVEL2_CACHE_*)`. The host's caches (Xeon 6975P-C, read on the runner) are L2 2 MiB 16-way, L3 480 MiB: neither leaf is the host's, and neither was before #570. *Options read and run:* `legacy-cache=off` is refused under `host` (`CPU model 'host' doesn't support legacy-cache=off`), since only a named model has a cache model of its own. `host-cache-info=on` passes the host's leaf 2 through, 0x00feff01, whose 0xFF sends glibc round leaf 4 again: CPUID exits 2371 → 4087, every exit +1693 [+1657, +1825] (lab 45101932224). No option keeps the descriptors and makes them agree. *Changed:* `qemu/patches/0006` (#594) adds `x-legacy-cpuid2-cache`, default on as QEMU is; off, leaf 2 encodes `env->cache_info` (0x2C, 0x30, 0x49, 0x4D). `-cpu` sets it off and `cpuidProperties` holds it, in the same record change as #569. *Measured* (bench, nested, spin's shape, against v20261123.03, whose leaves disagree, with ctl = #569's spin-machine on the patched QEMU): leaf 2 EDX 0x2c3049, ECX 0x4d, and glibc's L2 4194304/16, the kernel's 4194304/16, its thresholds unchanged (labs 45101932202, 45101932412). CPUID exits 2371 → 2406-2449, leaf 2 and leaf 4 still 12 and 4 a process: two more processes in the fix's boots, not a dearer one (inferred); every exit +13 [−143, +96] (3 a side). First login +0.6 [−6.1, +5.5] against the release, +0.4 [−7.5, +5.8] for ctl, 40 a side (lab 45101932173). Restored login evicted −2.1 [−6.1, +5.2], cached −1.7 [−4.3, +1.7], 30 a side (lab 45101932449). *Compatibility:* no release's QEMU has the switch, so the record refuses every release's checkpoints by it; #569 already refused every checkpoint a released spin-machine wrote, so spin's checkpoints boot cold once. `boot:compat` refuses v20261123.03, the newest release, first: `cpu x-legacy-cpuid2-cache: checkpoint none, here false (same)` (CI run 45104443218). *Status:* in place.
