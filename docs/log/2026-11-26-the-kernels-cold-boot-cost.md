*Why:* report.yml flagged v20261126.01's cold boot to first login at +7.7 [+1.4, +16.7] ms against v20261124.01 (20 a side, run 45819169536), and the rule (docs/releasing.md, "Withdrawing a release") asks whether that is noise, an accepted cost, or a regression. *Measured:* it is real. Booted again by report.yml (run 45824304385) the goal row read +8.3 [−1.2, +12.6], within noise alone, but the same run's other cold logins did not: prestart's cold boot +6.5 [+1.5, +9.4] and into a QEMU started ahead +10.8 [+4.5, +15.5]. Restore to first login did not move in either run (−0.4 [−1.0, +0.8], +0.1 [−0.9, +0.6]), and the kernel to "Freeing" moved +1.0 and +0.5 ms, so the cost lands after the kernel hands over, in the boot's userspace before sshd. Taken apart by part on the bench (ssh-first, spin's shape and login, 30 a side, lab 45824729980), v20261124.01 with only v20261126.01's kernel read +14.2 [+8.1, +18.2], with only its QEMU +4.1 [−0.9, +8.2], within noise, and v20261124.01's image with every other part new +9.7 [+3.0, +15.4], so neither the image nor QEMU 11.2.0-rc1, swtpm, passt or pc-q35-11.2 owns it. Taken apart by kernel, each from a parts.yml run, with each step's Δ bootstrapped from the run's samples against the step before it, the step from 7.4-rc4 to shirudo's hooks, configuration and lockdown floor first read +10.3 [+6.3, +19.6] (lab 45825554610, a noisier host), and split into its three commits on a quieter one none of them moved: hooks −0.8, configuration +3.8, floor +0.6, and that kernel at confidentiality against integrity +0.6, all within noise (lab 45826674842). At 50 a side on one host (lab 45827437575) the staircase from v20261124.01 is: 7.3.6 to 7.4-rc4 +6.6 [−1.4, +10.8], shirudo's hooks, configuration and floor −1.3, RD_ZSTD, FPROBE and the rest of shirudo's flow +1.6, signed modules +0.1; the whole kernel +7.2 [−0.5, +13.9], and v20261126.01 against it +1.1 [−6.2, +5.3], the release against v20261124.01 +8.4 [+0.3, +12.2]. The kernel owns the cost, most of it on the move to 7.4-rc4, and no one commit's share clears this bench's noise. *Decided:* accepted. Each step is a kernel shirudo needs (the BPF signed loader's ABI 7.3 lacks, its hooks and configuration, lockdown at integrity for its reads) or signed-only modules, and none has a smaller form that keeps what it is for. v20261126.01 is not withdrawn. *Not known:* what in 7.4-rc4 makes the boot's userspace slower, which a ledger of each kernel, 7.3.6 against 7.4-rc4, would show, and 7.4 final measured against this release (#768). *Status:* the cost stands in every release from v20261126.01.
